LAPIDARY CONSOLE

WELCOME

Lapidary sets up AI on this PC and makes it useful on day one. Nothing happens until you say so, and every step says what it does.

You can come back here any time from 1 Start. The Advanced switch in the top bar shows the details on every screen.

WHICH AI SHOULD WE USE?

There is a free one that runs on this PC, there are online ones that cost money per use, and you can mix them.

WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE?

Pick one. Each one installs and connects only what it needs, and tells you what it did. You can pick another afterwards.

HERE IS WHAT I WILL DO

    Keys come from your own Anthropic or OpenAI account and are paid by use. They are stored with the operating system's credential encryption and never shown again.
