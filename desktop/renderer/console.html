WELCOME

Lapidary sets up AI on this PC and makes it useful on day one. Nothing happens until you say so, and every step says what it does.

            CONNECT THE PIECES
            
          
            Your prompts and assistants live in Lapidary. Connecting an app means that app can use them too. Each button writes one config file for one app and says what it wrote.
            
          
            
            
            Claude Desktop
            Lets Claude Desktop use your prompts, your assistants and REA. Restart the app afterwards. Adds lapidary and rea to mcpServers in claude_desktop_config.json; other entries are kept. 
            
            
            ChatGPT
            The same for ChatGPT, where the app supports connections on this PC. Writes the same entries to ChatGPT's mcp_config.json. 
            
            
            Over the network
            Lapidary also answers as an MCP server at an address, for apps that take one: Claude Desktop and Claude Code connectors, Cursor, VS Code, and ChatGPT when the address is reachable from the internet (a tunnel such as Cloudflare Tunnel or ngrok, with the tunnel's host listed in Advanced settings). The address needs your Lapidary key as the X-API-Key header.   For an app that only starts local commands, the mcp-remote entry bridges it to the address.
            
            
            Open WebUI
            Starts the private chat on this PC, with your prompts, the online AI and the one on this PC all available in it. Needs Docker Desktop. Writes open-webui.compose.yml with Lapidary, OpenAI and Ollama as providers and runs docker compose up on port 3080. 
            
          
            
          
            
        
            
      
            

      
            
        
            
          
            WHICH AI, KEYS AND STORAGE
            
          
            Which AI answers, how it is paid for, and where your prompts are kept. The free one on this PC costs nothing and keeps everything here; the online ones are smarter and billed to your own account.
